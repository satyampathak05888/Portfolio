Spotify Tracks: Statistical Analysis

Statistics 1 extra activity | Google Sheets

I picked a random sample of 5,000 tracks from the public Spotify Tracks dataset and studied 9 variables: 3 categorical (Explicit, Mode, Key) and 6 numerical (Popularity, Duration, Danceability, Energy, Loudness, Tempo).

  • About 65% of tracks are in major mode and about 9% are explicit.
  • Track duration is right-skewed because a few very long tracks pull the mean up.
  • Energy and loudness show a strong positive relationship (correlation about 0.75).

Statistics 1

Statistics 1 is a foundation course of the IIT Madras BS Data Science program. It teaches how to collect, organise and summarise data with tables, graphs and measures like mean and median. It then introduces probability, random variables and common distributions. The course builds the base for data analysis and for later courses like Statistics 2.
